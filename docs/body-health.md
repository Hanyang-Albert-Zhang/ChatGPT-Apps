
# Body measurements and wellness rating
Body Measurements is reachable from Goals & settings and a direct hash route, not listed in either navigation panel. The saved profile selects the male blue or female pink figure and chest/bust label. Four circumference fields use inches. Each field has one authenticated, server-stamped insert per Eastern-time Monday week. Unique deterministic row IDs and INSERT ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING enforce the limit even across tabs/concurrent requests. Owned PATCH updates values without changing week/key; DELETE marks a weekly entry as removed. The original slot remains used until the next week. Removed data is excluded from trends and health context. Retired High Hip records are preserved in storage but excluded from the API, UI, and scoring. Ordinary records endpoints exclude these private measurements and cannot delete them through the generic endpoint; friends and shares never receive them.
History retains one record per field/week, with full tables and per-field time-series charts. Changes and least-squares slope use elapsed weeks, preserving gaps. They describe trends without assigning an ideal body shape.
Overall Health Rating is an explicitly app-defined, non-clinical wellness habit index. The last 28 days supply capped movement (30%), strength-date frequency (20%), age/sex-specific fiber (20%), weight-relative protein (20%), and saved daily calorie-goal alignment (10%). A grade requires all five components, saved profile, at least 14 food dates, and exercise records. Missing data is displayed, not silently considered measured zero intake. Grade thresholds match the requested SSS through F bands. Absolute lifted pounds never earn grade points.
BMI, estimated body fat, waist-to-height/hip ratio, all-time PRs, exercise minutes/miles and history appear alongside the score. BMI-derived body fat is clearly an estimate (Deurenberg study; approx 4.1 percentage-point error), not double-counted in the grade. Ordinary logs cannot establish VO2max, body composition, or normative strength/stamina/endurance levels. All formulas, assumptions and sources are disclosed in the modal.

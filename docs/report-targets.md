
# Report chart references
Every nutrition chart compares bucket totals with the sum of current daily references for the same calendar days. Calories respect the saved daily target and zigzag budget; when no target is saved, a complete profile supplies the existing Mifflin–St Jeor estimate. Protein uses the lower end of current US guidance (1.2 g/kg/day); fiber uses National Academies age/sex references (men through age 50:38g; older:30g; women through50:25g; older:21g). Missing profile inputs produce no invented target.
The exercise-time red reference is 150/7 minutes per day, summing to 150 per week. It is the HHS adult moderate-aerobic guideline, common to both sexes. The existing chart includes all logged exercise, so notes distinguish its minutes from proven aerobic guideline adherence. Strength frequency guidance is two days/week.
Lifted volume = sets × reps × external load in lb. Two Hands is total combined load; One Hand is one hand's external load. Legacy perHand two-dumbbell records double load; kg converts to lb. Cardio/bodyweight contribute no external load.
NASM does not specify a universal daily pounds-lifted requirement by sex. Its guidance is goal-dependent sets/reps/intensity. The weight chart's red reference is explicitly the saved FitDiet training plan's estimated external volume, not a USDA/NASM health threshold. It starts at the saved plan start date and requires starting load. Plan sets × reps × kg converted to lb; assumes two dumbbells except single-arm, concentration, goblet and kettlebell movements; excludes holds without reps. Custom plan prescriptions are app programming choices informed by NASM and existing source references.
Reference lines are horizontal within each bucket, with steps where totals differ (month length, partial weeks, zigzag daily goals, plan changes). Their values, units and source explanations remain visible. Current profile goals are applied to historical chart dates; this is not a historical snapshot of past goals.
Sources: https://realfood.gov/ ; https://www.nationalacademies.org/news/report-offers-new-eating-and-physical-activity-targets-to-reduce-chronic-disease-risk ; https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html ; https://www.nasm.org/certified-personal-trainer/the-opt-model .
Verification: node scripts/check-report-targets.mjs covers leap months, partial weeks, zigzag budgets, age/sex/body-weight references, load conversions and plan availability.

## Strength-minute volume (current workout chart)
The previous pounds-lifted chart is replaced by total logged strength minutes, including bodyweight. Movement type classifies records; legacy exercise-name fallback is retained. Cardio is excluded. References sum strength and hold prescription minutes from the saved FitDiet plan, require a plan start date, and do not require any external starting load. Distinct logged strength dates and average dates/week appear next to the guideline of at least two strength days weekly. Counts do not establish coverage or intensity. WHO/CDC prescribe strength frequency, not a universal minute quota. NASM and ACE articles are linked for goal-dependent routine dose, sets/reps and progression.
Sources: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128 ; https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html ; https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/training/strength-training-routines-beginners ; https://www.acefitness.org/continuing-education/certified/august-2023/8398/the-ultimate-ace-workout-an-evidence-based-approach-to-programming-and-class-design/ .
